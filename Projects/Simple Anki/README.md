## **Project Overview**

The project is an interactive, web-based vocabulary learning platform inspired by flashcard tools like Anki. It is designed specifically for English language learners worldwide by delivering **100% English-only content**—allowing students of any native language to learn and navigate the platform seamlessly without requiring translation.

---

## **Key Features & System Architecture**

### **1. Vocabulary Categorization & Progression System**

* **CEFR Standardized Levels:** The database categorizes English vocabulary according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR):
* $A1$ (Beginner)
* $A2$ (Elementary)
* $B1$ (Intermediate)
* $B2$ (Upper-Intermediate)
* $C1$ (Advanced)
* $C2$ (Proficient)


* **Structured Progression:**
* Students complete short, daily study sessions (e.g., 5 minutes per day) within a specific CEFR level.
* To unlock or advance to the next level, students must achieve a minimum accuracy threshold (e.g., **greater than 70% correct**).



---

### **2. Flashcard Design & User Experience**

Each flashcard is dual-sided and built to maximize visual and auditory comprehension:

#### **Front Side**

* Displays only the **target word** in English to allow the student to attempt recall.

#### **Back Side** *(Revealed upon turning the card)*

* **Target Word:** Displayed prominently.
* **Visual Aid:** An image clearly depicting the meaning of the word.
* **Audio Pronunciation:** An interactive audio button that plays the spoken pronunciation of the word when clicked.
* **Phonetic Transcription:** The word written in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) displayed alongside the audio button.
* **English Definition:** A concise, easy-to-understand English description sourced from a dictionary (e.g., for *car*: *"A vehicle with four wheels..."*).

---

### **3. Student Scoring & Personal Review Pool**

* **Correct Answers:**
* If the student correctly identifies/recalls a word, no changes are made to their review list, and they move toward completing their daily target/level accuracy threshold.


* **Incorrect Answers:**
* If the student gets a word wrong, that word is automatically flagged and saved to their **Personal Review Pool** ("Words to Be Reviewed") within their account.


* **Dedicated Review Mode:**
* Students can access a dedicated review area at any time to focus specifically on the words they find most challenging, allowing for targeted practice and reinforcement.
